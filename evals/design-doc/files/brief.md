# Brief: smarter "Top pick" badges

From: Product, marketplace search

Today a listing gets the "Top pick" badge when its rating is 4.8 or more and it sold at least 50 units in the last 90 days. The badge shows on search results and on the listing page, for every buyer, and is updated live. Sellers watch it closely: it is the first thing they ask about when sales dip.

We think the rule misses good listings (new ones never reach 50 sales) and keeps tired ones. We would like a model to decide which listings get the badge, so it goes to the listings buyers will be happiest with. We'd like it live by the end of the quarter.

A sample of 3,000 listings, with what each sold in the 30 days after the sample was taken, is in `data/listings_sample.csv`.
