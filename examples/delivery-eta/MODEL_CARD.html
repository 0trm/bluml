Use

What the product does with the prediction, and where not to use it

Intended use: a 10-minute window at checkout; its upper end, P80, is the promise.
Out of scope: new cities and restaurants under 2 weeks of orders (they get the rule's estimate); ranking restaurants.
Evaluation

Which rows it was judged on, by which measure, and for which groups

Evaluation data: September orders by checkout time, used once.
Metrics: repeat orders at 30 days → orders inside the promise → tickets, conversion → pinball@0.8.
Factors: peak hours and rain hold the gain; restaurants under 2 weeks 74% vs 70%, interval crosses zero; trips over 6 km too few to judge.
Training

The data it learned from, and the runs it beat on August orders

Training data: orders March to July, one per checkout; labels 197 of 200 match courier GPS; 3 post-checkout fields dropped.
Risks

Who the model can harm, and the errors it still makes

Ethical considerations: the promise shapes demand and courier load; new cities get a less accurate promise; no customer attributes in the inputs.
Caveats: late orders left come from queues that jump after checkout and stacked orders; decays about 1 point a month, retrained monthly.
